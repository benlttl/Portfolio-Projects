# Portfolio Projects

### Aros Media: Content Performance & Profitability Dashboard
**Link :** 

**Goal :** Evaluate content performance and financial efficiency to identify profitable products, creators, accounts, and content strategies, and provide data-driven recommendations for allocating production resources.

**Description :** Developed a content performance dashboard and management financial analysis using a dataset of more than 2,000 records. Analyzed production costs, gross merchandise value (GMV), commissions, orders, profit, and return on investment (ROI). Built a management profit and loss statement, unit economics analysis, and expense breakdown to evaluate profitability and identify areas for operational improvement.

**Skills :** Data cleaning and validation; KPI development; profitability analysis; unit economics; ROI analysis; expense analysis; data visualization; business intelligence; financial modeling; management reporting; translating findings into business recommendations.

**Technology :** Microsoft Excel, spreadsheet formulas, pivot tables, charts, and dashboard reporting.

**Results :** Built a consolidated view of content performance and business profitability, making it possible to compare performance across creators, products, accounts, and content styles. The management P&L identified $200.64 in commission revenue, $1,965.97 in total expenses, and a $1,765.33 operating loss for the period analyzed.

**Recommendations :** 
* Increase content production for products such as JOYMODE Ignite Libido Support, IRIS Hormone Health Drink Mix, BetterAlt Shilajit Honey Sticks, tarte CC Color-Correct Tinted Serum, and Beast Bites Creatine Gummies based on their strong profit and ROI performance.
* Increase production for high-performing creators with consistently positive ROI, such as Blakely.
* Product Overlay was the only profitable content style; prioritize this format while reducing investment in consistently negative-ROI styles.
* Consider gradually increasing video production volume while monitoring whether additional output continues to improve profitability.
* Replicate the characteristics and content styles of the top 10 videos videos in future production.
* Evaluate subscription costs as they account for approximately 31% of total expenses.


### Voltadrive: Financial Modeling & Three-Statement Analysis
**Link :** 

**Goal :** Construct an integrated set of financial statements for a hypothetical manufacturing business and evaluate its profitability, liquidity, leverage, asset efficiency, and cash flow.

**Description :** Developed an income statement, balance sheet, and statement of cash flows for a first-year truck manufacturer. Modeled manufacturing costs, cost of goods sold, depreciation, equipment transactions, debt, interest expense, and retained earnings. Performed financial ratio analysis to assess the company's operating performance and financial position, and evaluated the implications of its financing and investment decisions.

**Skills :** Financial statement preparation; integrated financial modeling; managerial accounting; cost of goods manufactured and sold; depreciation accounting; cash flow analysis; profitability ratios; liquidity analysis; leverage analysis; working capital analysis; financial interpretation; model reconciliation.

**Technology :** Microsoft Excel, spreadsheet formulas, financial statement schedules, and ratio analysis.

**Results :** Produced a reconciled financial model showing $75.0 million in sales, $5.1 million in operating income, and $4.05 million in net income under the model's assumptions. 

Ratio analysis identified:
* Elevated leverage and short-term liquidity risk
* Positive operating cash flow
* Negative free cash flow driven by substantial investment

These findings illustrate the trade-offs between growth investment, debt financing, profitability, and liquidity.


### Robinhood Financial Health & Industry Benchmark Analysis
**Link :** 

**Goal :** Assess Robinhood's financial condition and operating performance by calculating key financial ratios and comparing results against industry benchmarks to identify strengths, weaknesses, and potential areas for improvement.

**Description :** Analyzed Robinhood's financial statements using reported company financial data and industry benchmark data. Calculated and interpreted liquidity, leverage, profitability, and asset efficiency ratios, including current ratio, quick ratio, debt ratio, equity multiplier, pre-tax profit margin, return on assets, return on equity, and total asset turnover. Compared company performance with industry benchmarks and developed recommendations based on the relative results.

**Skills :** Financial statement analysis; financial ratio calculations; industry benchmarking; comparative analysis; profitability assessment; liquidity and solvency analysis; asset utilization analysis; financial research; interpretation of financial performance; business writing and recommendations.

**Technology :** Microsoft Excel, financial statement data, industry benchmark reports, and financial ratio models.

**Results :** Identified a mixed financial performance profile. 
* Robinhood's pre-tax profit margin of 47.13% exceeded the 18.1% industry benchmark
* Its pre-tax return on assets of 5.53% and return on equity of 23.04% were below their respective benchmarks of 16.7% and 42.6%.
* Its current ratio of 1.26 was also below the benchmark of 2.3
* Total asset turnover of 0.117 was substantially below the benchmark of 1.7

The analysis highlighted strong pre-tax margins alongside potential weaknesses in liquidity and asset utilization.
